Privacy Policy for Baby Piano: Kids Music Games

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

TinyTunes Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application Baby Piano: Kids Music Games (Application ID: com.tinytunes.babypiano.kidsmusic). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of young children and their parents.

1. COPPA & Family Policy Compliance

Our application is designed specifically for children under the age of 13, toddlers, and families. We strictly adhere to:

  • The United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • The European Union GDPR-K regulations for minors.
  • Google Play's Designed for Families Policy.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, GPS coordinates, photos, voice recordings, or device identifiers.

Gameplay progress (stars collected, unlocked train pets, language preference) is stored strictly locally on your device using local sandboxed storage and is never transmitted to any remote servers.

3. Kid-Safe Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application 100% free for families worldwide without requiring real-money in-app purchases:

  • All ads served via Google AdMob are explicitly flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true.
  • All ads are strictly filtered to G-rated content suitable for all children.
  • No behavioral profiling, ad personalization, or tracking cookies are utilized.

4. Parental Gate Protection

All adult-oriented sections (grown-up settings, external store reviews) are shielded behind a multiplication/addition Parental Gate, preventing children from leaving the safe game environment.
